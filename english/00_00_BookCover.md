### About the Book
Artificial intelligence is rewriting how businesses operate, and the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam (V1.1, April 2026) now covers a much broader surface than the original release. Foundation models, agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and AWS-managed agent platforms have moved from "interesting research" to "tested material" in just over a year.

This guide prepares you for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam while delivering the practical knowledge to drive AI initiatives inside your organization. You will learn how to evaluate AI options, communicate confidently with engineering teams, and pick the right AWS service for the right problem.

- Build a solid grasp of AI, ML, and generative AI concepts and where each one fits a business problem
- Get up to speed on the latest in-scope AWS services, including Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Strands Agents, Kiro, and Amazon Quick
- Understand responsible AI, governance, and the AWS Generative AI Security Scoping Matrix
- Learn how to secure AI systems, from IAM and encryption to Bedrock Guardrails and prompt-injection defenses
- Prepare confidently for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) exam
- Apply the material to real projects, not just exam questions

### Who This Book Is For
This guide is written for business analysts, IT support professionals, marketing specialists, product managers, and other non-builders who need a working understanding of AI on AWS. No deep coding, math, or ML engineering experience is required.
