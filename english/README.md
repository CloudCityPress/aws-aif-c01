## About this edition

A comprehensive, exam-aligned study guide written for business professionals: analysts, project managers, product managers, and IT staff who need a working understanding of AI and generative AI on AWS without diving into low-level implementation details.

Every chapter is structured around the V1.1 exam guide's five domains and fourteen task statements, so study time maps directly onto what the exam evaluates.
