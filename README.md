# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01): A Business Professional's Guide
by Vladimir Provorov

> **Free English and Spanish editions for personal learning.**
> Third Edition (May 2026) — rewritten from scratch for the V1.1 exam guide (April 30, 2026).

## About This Book

A comprehensive study guide for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) certification, written for business professionals: analysts, project managers, product managers, and IT staff who need to understand AI and generative AI on AWS without diving into low-level implementation details.

Every chapter is structured around the V1.1 exam guide's five domains and fourteen task statements, so study time maps directly onto what the exam evaluates.

**New in the third edition (V1.1 coverage):**
- Agentic AI concepts and business use cases
- Amazon Bedrock AgentCore for production agent deployments
- Strands Agents and the open-source agent framework
- Kiro for spec-driven AI development
- Amazon Quick for natural-language business intelligence
- References to retired services (such as Amazon MemoryDB for AI workloads) removed

**What you will learn:**
- Foundational AI, ML, and generative AI concepts in business contexts
- How to identify and evaluate AI opportunities inside an organization
- Selecting foundation models, prompt engineering, RAG, and fine-tuning fundamentals
- Best practices for delivering and managing AI projects on AWS
- Responsible AI, model governance, and AI security on AWS
- The AWS AI service portfolio: Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Q, AgentCore, Strands Agents, Kiro, Amazon Quick

**Exam preparation:**
- All five domains and fourteen task statements of AIF-C01 V1.1, in the order the official guide presents them
- Self-check questions at the end of each chapter for active recall
- Real-world scenarios that test understanding of AI concepts in business settings
